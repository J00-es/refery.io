Candidate fit
Who gets matched
CS degree from top school Strong signal MIT, Stanford, CMU, Berkeley, Caltech, ETH Zürich, and other universities known for CS/engineering. Top-ranked programs signal technical rigor to our clients.
Big Tech or well-known startup experience Strong signal Google, Meta, Apple, Amazon, Microsoft, Stripe, Airbnb, OpenAI, Anthropic, and similar. Known tech logos carry weight with early-stage founders.
Startup experience is a huge bonus Strong signal Early-stage company experience (seed, Series A) is particularly valued. It shows adaptability, ownership, and comfort with ambiguity — exactly what our clients need.
2–10 years of experience is the sweet spot Senior enough to contribute immediately, early enough to grow with the company. These profiles have the highest placement rate across our client base.
Salary expectations: $200K–$350K base Plus equity and bonus, depending on experience. Most of our open roles sit in this range. Candidates expecting significantly below or above may have fewer matches.
Work authorization required (US) Note Very few of our clients offer visa sponsorship. Candidates should have valid US work authorization (US citizen, green card holder, or valid work permit). Flag this early.
Recent graduates — lower match rate Note New grads and those under 2 years of experience are harder to place given the seniority profile most clients are hiring for. They can still be submitted, but set expectations accordingly.

Process
How it works
1
Send us your top profiles
Email your candidate list with their LinkedIn profiles to candidates@refery.io. Start with 5–10 of your strongest profiles. No lengthy forms, no onboarding call required.
2
We review and identify fits
Our team reviews your submissions against the current opportunity set and comes back to you with the candidates we'd like to move forward with. Only the strongest matches.
3
You make the warm intro
For selected candidates, we ask you to do a warm introduction — a quick personal note connecting the candidate to Refery. That's the trust signal that makes our process work.
4
We take it from here
Our team has a quick call with the candidate, verifies fit, and begins matching with clients. You don't need to do anything after the intro. We manage the entire process end-to-end.
5
Get paid after placement
If the candidate is hired, your referral bonus is paid out 3 months after their start date. Standard industry cliff — ensures a quality placement.
