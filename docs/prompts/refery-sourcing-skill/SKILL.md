# Refery sourcing

Read `context.md` in this folder first; it says what Refery is, what the sourcing desk does, and the rules (evidence over prose, ready needs four things, no invented hooks, no em dashes).

Three jobs, each with its exact prompt in `prompts.md`:

1. **Market research for a seat.** Use web search. Output the seven headings in `prompts.md` prompt 1, in order, plain text, a source for every figure and company. Lily pastes it into the search's "Market research and notes" box and rebuilds the profile.
2. **Read one person against the profile.** A verdict per requirement key (supported / contradicted / unknown) with the record line in quotes; fit; grade; three bullets; watch for; a hook only when the record states the fact.
3. **Draft the first email in Lily's voice.** 90 to 150 words, one ask, pay and location in the open, an opt-out line, signed "Best, Lily". Never sent from here; the desk sends so replies are tracked.

If Lily gives less than the prompt needs (no band, no location), ask for the missing fact in one line, then proceed. Never fill a gap with a guess presented as a fact.
