# How AI-native recruiting players brief founders in 2026, and what it means for the Refery founder brief

Research run 7 September 2026 (about 45 searches and page fetches). Every claim carries its source. Compiled to inform the redesign of refery.xyz/b/<slug>.

## The one-paragraph read

The 2025 to 2026 cohort (Perfectly, Contrario, Wellfound Autopilot, Dover) does not ask founders to read a document. Intake is a 45-minute call, a 5-minute voice brief, or a pasted JD; calibration happens by decision, not by prose: the founder approves or rejects real profiles in Slack, daily or weekly, and the system learns. The longest founder-side artefact found is Paraform's nine-section role form, which is the outlier. On the reading side, GOV.UK, NN/g and Vercel's writing guide all say the same thing: first two paragraphs carry everything, one action per screen, fold the rest, write the way you talk. Founders sign on phones (60% of e-signatures are mobile; half complete in under 15 minutes). The brief should therefore lead with the founder's to-do list, keep the actions in the thumb zone, and fold everything explanatory to its "in short" line.

## Fifteen insights, each with a source

1. Open with agreed versus open. Metaview's template gives every section two columns. That is the brief's job: here is what we understood, here is what we still need. [Metaview](https://www.metaview.ai/resources/blog/recruiter-intake-meeting-questions-template)
2. Ask "who did you last say no to, and why", not for adjectives. Both Metaview and Paraform say specific rejections calibrate faster than requirement lists. [Metaview](https://www.metaview.ai/resources/blog/recruiter-intake-meeting-questions-template), [Paraform](https://knowledge.paraform.com/articles/6254741269-para-ai)
3. Cap the questions at three to five and make them concrete: what they ship in 90 days, which requirement you would trade, the honest pitch. [Paraform](https://knowledge.paraform.com/articles/7550953773-client-onboarding)
4. Two example profiles beat a paragraph of requirements. Paraform's highest-weighted section is 4 to 5 example LinkedIn profiles. [Paraform](https://knowledge.paraform.com/articles/7550953773-client-onboarding)
5. Binary decisions with a one-line reason: Approve, Not a fit, Skip. Tell the founder the click only calibrates. [Juicebox](https://docs.juicebox.ai/hiring-manager-seats)
6. Put the decision where the founder already is: Slack. Slack Connect cut client response time from 7 days to 3 in its own case studies. [Dover](https://www.dover.com/case-studies/section4), [Slack](https://slack.com/blog/collaboration/strengthen-client-relationships-slack-channels)
7. State cadence and SLA in numbers, both ways: Wellfound (48h calibration, 5 to 10 a week), Paraform (24h each way). [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com/recruit/all-features/autopilot), [Paraform](https://www.paraform.com/blog/tips-for-role-calibration-between-recruiters-and-hiring-managers)
8. Front-load. GOV.UK: the first two paragraphs must carry the most important information; people read 20 to 28% of a page. Vercel: every page opens with a one-paragraph TL;DR, sentences under 20 words. [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/content-design/writing-for-gov-uk), [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-people-read-online/), [Vercel](https://github.com/vercel-labs/writing-guidelines/blob/main/command.md)
9. Accordions on mobile: collapsed by default, descriptive headers, never fold what most readers need (the actions, the questions). [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mobile-accordions/)
10. One primary action per screen, in the thumb zone. Sticky bottom CTAs lift completion 3 to 7% in published tests. [Smashing](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2019/08/bottom-navigation-pattern-mobile-web-pages/), [Digital X](https://www.digitalxlabs.io/blogs/web-design/sticky-cta-design)
11. Signing is a mobile act: 60% of signatures on mobile, 76% of agreements complete within a day, half within 15 minutes. [Dropbox Sign](https://sign.dropbox.com/blog/faster-smarter-more-secure-how-dropbox-sign-is-accelerating-business-in-2025)
12. Write in spoken language. Paul Graham: "write in spoken language". [PG](https://paulgraham.com/talk.html)
13. Show the founder the exact three-line pitch you will use and let them edit it. [Metaview](https://www.metaview.ai/resources/blog/recruiter-intake-meeting-questions-template)
14. Time-box blocks visibly ("3 min"). Paraform labels every section with minutes. [Paraform](https://knowledge.paraform.com/articles/7550953773-client-onboarding)
15. Treat the brief as a living record with a stable URL, not a one-shot document. [SignalFire](https://www.signalfire.com/blog/startup-hiring-plan)

## The information architecture we adopted on 7 September

Above the fold on a phone: a compact title with read time; a "your part" checklist derived from the page (sign, Slack, delivery choice, N questions) that ticks itself as answers land; two lines of context. Then the sections as folds that open to their "in short" line, with "How we work" and the questions open by default and everything else folded. A sticky bar on phones keeps the primary action and the questions under the thumb. Comments stay under every section.

Not yet adopted, worth considering next: a "who did you last say no to" question on every brief; two example profiles per role as "looks like this"; stating our own reply SLA in hours; and a changelog line when a brief is updated after a comment.
